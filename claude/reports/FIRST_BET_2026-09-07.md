## Why USGS and not GDELT

The debut was moved off GDELT (Kimi R41). GDELT's daily event count **halves at
weekends** — 117,020 Thu / 107,037 Fri / 66,878 Sat — so beating naive persistence there
is calendar arithmetic, not insight. Earthquakes have no weekly cycle: 2026-09-02..06
reads **17 / 24 / 12 / 13 / 15** across Wed–Sun, and the weekend is the *high* end.
Persistence is a true ceiling here. The GDELT wiring (4c6751d) is untouched and stays
prepared for the second bet.

## THREE DEFECTS IN THIS RUN, none of which I can fix retroactively

**1. The model is `qwen3:8b`, not the 3B the spec names.** `core.brain.think()` chose
the 8B on the ladder's local leg and `first_bet` did not pin a model. The spec says
"N=8 completions from the local 3B". This bet was generated by a stronger model than
specified, and that changes what it measures. Recorded in `llm_provenance.jsonl`,
caller `brain:forecaster`, 8 rows, all `qwen3:8b`, 1004.5 s total.

**2. It is not best-of-N from ONE prompt.** The spec says "ONE prompt, N=8
completions". Provenance shows **four distinct `prompt_sha` values** across the eight
calls — `26f79966` ×1, `c0798c89` ×3, `ba2e7ab1` ×2, `0f622bbd` ×2. `brain.think()`
folds rotating context into the prompt, so this is eight samples from a forecaster
whose prompt drifts, not a best-of-N draw from a fixed one. The selection ("smallest
confidence width") therefore ranges over candidates that were not asked the same
question.

**3. `V0_date` in the ledger says `2026-09-07`. It is wrong.** The value 15 is the
count for the UTC day **2026-09-06** — `main()` writes `date.today()` instead of the
registered observation date. The *value* is right and the seal hash does not cover
`V0_date`, so the bet itself is unaffected; the label would mislead anyone grading it
later, which is why it is stated here rather than quietly corrected in a sealed file.

**None of the three is fixed by editing the sealed artefact.** A bet that gets edited
after sealing is not a bet.

## PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION, from the spec, before grading

> "the first single bet is roughly a coin-flip against persistence on a daily event
> count — P(system error < persistence error on bet #1) ≈ 0.5. The signal that means
> something is TWO different indicators beaten, not one."

**My own reading, on record now:** I expect persistence to win this one. The series
is 17 / 24 / 12 / 13 / 15 — a mean near 16 with day-to-day swings of ±5, and the sealed
bet is **+3, the largest delta any candidate proposed**. Predicting a jump on a series
that looks like noise around its own mean is the bet most likely to lose to "tomorrow =
today". **P(system error < persistence error) ≈ 0.35**, below the spec's 0.5, and I
would rather be on record low than claim the coin-flip after seeing the number.

## Two risks that will affect grading

**Magnitude revision.** 2 of the 15 events on 2026-09-06 sit within 0.1 of the M4.5
threshold, and USGS demonstrably revises: max `updated − time` on that day is **24.7
hours**. A day's count can change after the fact. **The grading step must record the
timestamp at which it read the count**, or the same bet grades differently on different
days.

**The day boundary.** The count is a **UTC calendar day**, never the rolling
`4.5_day.geojson` window. Grading must use
`starttime=2026-09-07T00:00:00&endtime=2026-09-08T00:00:00&minmagnitude=4.5`.
